Brazil: election results by polling station

1998–2026 · 8 elections · BRA

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Brazil from 8 elections between 1998 and 2026: 1998, 2002, 2006, 2010, 2014, 2018, 2022 and 2026. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2026 election has 94,539 polling stations, and 99% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Brazil", years = 2026)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Brazil", years=[2026])
