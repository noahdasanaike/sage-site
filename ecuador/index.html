Ecuador: election results by parish

2002–2025 · 8 elections · ECU

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Ecuador from 8 elections between 2002 and 2025: 2002, 2006, 2009, 2013, 2017, 2021, 2023 and 2025. The smallest unit is the parish. The 2025 election has 1,301 parishs, and 95% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.
