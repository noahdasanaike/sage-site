Moldova: election results by polling station

2014–2025 · 7 elections · MDA

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Moldova from 7 elections between 2014 and 2025: 2014, 2016, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2024 and 2025. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2025 election has 2,274 polling stations, and 86% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.
