Nicaragua: election results by polling station

2001 · 1 election · NIC

SAGE has presidential election results for Nicaragua from 2001. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2001 election has 9,453 polling stations, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.
