Ukraine: election results by polling station

2019 · 1 election · UKR

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Ukraine from 2019. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2019 election has 29,989 polling stations, and 99% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Ukraine", years = 2019)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Ukraine", years=[2019])
