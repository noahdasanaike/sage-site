Taiwan: election results by polling station

1995–2024 · 11 elections · TWN

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Taiwan from 11 elections between 1995 and 2024: 1995, 1996, 1998, 2000, 2001, 2004, 2008, 2012, 2016, 2020 and 2024. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2024 election has 17,795 polling stations, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for 2020 and 2024.

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.
