United States of America: election results by precinct

2016–2024 · 3 elections · USA

SAGE has presidential election results for the United States of America from 3 elections between 2016 and 2024: 2016, 2020 and 2024. The smallest unit is the precinct. The 2024 election has 167,549 precincts, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("United States of America", years = 2024)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("United States of America", years=[2024])
