Senegal: election results by polling station

2024 · 1 election · SEN

SAGE has legislative election results for Senegal from 2024. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2024 election has 10,932 polling stations, and 98% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Senegal", years = 2024)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Senegal", years=[2024])
