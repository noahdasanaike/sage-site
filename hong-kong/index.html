Hong Kong: election results by polling station

2016–2021 · 2 elections · HKG

SAGE has legislative election results for Hong Kong from 2 elections between 2016 and 2021: 2016 and 2021. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2021 election has 656 polling stations, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.
