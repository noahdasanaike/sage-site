Isle of Man: election results by constituency

2016–2026 · 3 elections · IMN

SAGE has legislative election results for the Isle of Man from 3 elections between 2016 and 2026: 2016, 2021 and 2026. The smallest unit is the constituency. The 2026 election has 12 constituencies, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Isle of Man", years = 2026)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Isle of Man", years=[2026])

Notes

  • House of Keys general elections in the 12 two-member constituencies of the 2013 boundaries, used unchanged in 2016, 2021 and 2026.
  • Each voter may cast up to two votes, so total_votes is the sum of candidate votes in the constituency and is roughly 1.7 to 1.9 times the number of voters. Votes are recorded by candidate; party is the ballot description on the Notice of Poll, and candidates without one are Independent.
  • The 2021 and 2026 figures are transcribed from hand-filled Notices of Result, read twice independently, and the two readings agree on every figure. No 2026 electorate by constituency was published, so reg and turnout_reg are missing for 2026.
  • Constituency polygons are the Isle of Man Government's House of Keys constituency boundaries.
