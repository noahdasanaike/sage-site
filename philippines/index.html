Philippines: election results by polling station

2022–2025 · 2 elections · PHL

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for the Philippines from 2 elections between 2022 and 2025: 2022 and 2025. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2025 election has 92,588 polling stations, and 86% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.
