Netherlands: election results by polling station

2010–2025 · 6 elections · NLD

SAGE has legislative election results for the Netherlands from 6 elections between 2010 and 2025: 2010, 2012, 2017, 2021, 2023 and 2025. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2025 election has 10,085 polling stations, and 96% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1, Eurostat NUTS), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Netherlands", years = 2025)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Netherlands", years=[2025])
