Serbia: election results by polling station

2000–2022 · 12 elections · SRB

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Serbia from 12 elections between 2000 and 2022: 2000, 2002, 2003, 2004, 2007, 2008, 2012, 2014, 2016, 2017, 2020 and 2022. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2022 election has 8,267 polling stations. Unit boundaries are available for 2017.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Serbia", years = 2022)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Serbia", years=[2022])
