Ivory Coast: election results by constituency

2025 · 1 election · CIV

SAGE has legislative election results for Ivory Coast from 2025. The smallest unit is the constituency. The 2025 election has 204 constituencies, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Ivory Coast", years = 2025)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Ivory Coast", years=[2025])
