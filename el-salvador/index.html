El Salvador: election results by polling station

2014–2021 · 4 elections · SLV

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for El Salvador from 4 elections between 2014 and 2021: 2014, 2018, 2019 and 2021. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2021 election has 1,595 polling stations, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.
