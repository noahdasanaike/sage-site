Guyana: election results by polling station

2015–2025 · 2 elections · GUY

SAGE has legislative election results for Guyana from 2 elections between 2015 and 2025: 2015 and 2025. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2025 election has 1,722 polling stations, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.
