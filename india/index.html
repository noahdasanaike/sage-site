India: election results by polling station

2019 · 1 election · IND

SAGE has legislative election results for India from 2019. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2019 election has 1,020,235 polling stations, and 65% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("India", years = 2019)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("India", years=[2019])

Sources

  • Respective State and Union Territory Election Commissions

Notes

  • Thiessen polygons are generated separately by region, given substantial variation in how results are reported, digitized, and geocoded across states and union territories.
  • Three supplementary shapefiles for India are provided: Thiessen polygons, 1km point buffer, and 5km point buffer.
