Malta: election results by electoral division

2003–2026 · 6 elections · MLT

SAGE has legislative election results for Malta from 6 elections between 2003 and 2026: 2003, 2008, 2013, 2017, 2022 and 2026. The smallest unit is the electoral division. The 2026 election has 13 electoral divisions. There are no unit boundaries for this country.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

In R or Python:

        sage::sage_load("Malta", years = 2026)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Malta", years=[2026])

Notes

  • Votes are first-count (first-preference) votes under the Single Transferable Vote, summed from candidates to parties. Later-count transfers are not recorded.
  • The 13 electoral divisions are the finest published grain. Ballot boxes are mixed within each division before counting, so no locality or polling-station results exist.
  • Geometry is Missing for all years. Division boundaries are redrawn before each election and are published only as street-level descriptions, not as maps or polygons.
  • total_votes is the division's published valid vote, and turnout_reg is valid votes over the revised electoral register, so it runs a few points below the official turnout, which counts all ballots cast.
