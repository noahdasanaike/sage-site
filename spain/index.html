Spain: election results by polling station

1982–2023 · 13 elections · ESP

SAGE has legislative election results for Spain from 13 elections between 1982 and 2023: 1982, 1986, 1989, 1993, 1996, 2000, 2004, 2008, 2011, 2015, 2016, 2019 and 2023. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2023 election has 60,386 polling stations, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for 2004, 2008, 2011, 2015, 2016, 2019 and 2023.

        sage::sage_load("Spain", years = 2023)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Spain", years=[2023])
