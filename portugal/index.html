Portugal: election results by parish

1976–2026 · 25 elections · PRT

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Portugal from 25 elections between 1976 and 2026: 1976, 1979, 1980, 1983, 1985, 1986, 1987, 1991, 1995, 1996, 1999, 2001, 2002, 2005, 2006, 2009, 2011, 2015, 2016, 2019, 2021, 2022, 2024, 2025 and 2026. The smallest unit is the parish. The 2026 election has 3,259 parishs, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Portugal", years = 2026)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Portugal", years=[2026])
