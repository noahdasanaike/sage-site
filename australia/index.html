Australia: election results by polling station

2004–2025 · 8 elections · AUS

SAGE has legislative election results for Australia from 8 elections between 2004 and 2025: 2004, 2007, 2010, 2013, 2016, 2019, 2022 and 2025. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2025 election has 8,940 polling stations, and 92% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Australia", years = 2025)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Australia", years=[2025])
