Afghanistan: election results by polling station

2018–2019 · 2 elections · AFG

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Afghanistan from 2 elections between 2018 and 2019: 2018 and 2019. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2019 election has 25,075 polling stations, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Afghanistan", years = 2019)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Afghanistan", years=[2019])
