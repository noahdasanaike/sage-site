Indonesia: election results by polling station

2019 · 1 election · IDN

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Indonesia from 2019. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2019 election has 806,079 polling stations, and 88% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

Notes

  • Villages are matched to boundaries via a two-step process, whereby the name of the village is geocoded and the resulting latitude-longitude are matched to village boundaries.
  • Identifier code NAME columns (i.e., NAME1_b, NAME2_b, etc.) change between 2019 and 2024 election, but the names of places themselves (provinsi, kota, kecamatan, kelurahan) are largely consistent across years.
  • Supplementary shapefiles for Indonesia are provided at the NAME4 (village) level.
