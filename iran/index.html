Iran: election results by county

2017 · 1 election · IRN

SAGE has presidential election results for Iran from 2017. The smallest unit is the county. The 2017 election has 430 counties, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

Notes

  • Iranian election results are purportedly reported by city, though in some instances (Chardavol specifically) the unit is above or below that level; furthermore, there appears to be some degree of undercounting in the results. Polygons are obtained by getting “city” centroids then constructing Thiessen polygons, so between the apparent result undercounting and the absence of formal unit boundaries there is more error than expected on average here.
