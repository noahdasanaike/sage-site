Bangladesh: election results by polling station

2018–2026 · 3 elections · BGD

SAGE has legislative election results for Bangladesh from 3 elections between 2018 and 2026: 2018, 2024 and 2026. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2026 election has 42,384 polling stations, and 95% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Bangladesh", years = 2026)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Bangladesh", years=[2026])
