Maldives: election results by ballot box

2024 · 1 election · MDV

SAGE has legislative election results for the Maldives from 2024. The smallest unit is the ballot box. The 2024 election has 8,709 ballot boxes, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.
