Italy: election results by municipality

1948–2022 · 19 elections · ITA

SAGE has legislative election results for Italy from 19 elections between 1948 and 2022: 1948, 1953, 1958, 1963, 1968, 1972, 1976, 1979, 1983, 1987, 1992, 1994, 1996, 2001, 2006, 2008, 2013, 2018 and 2022. The smallest unit is the municipality. The 2022 election has 7,916 municipalities, and 96% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for 2001, 2006, 2008, 2013, 2018 and 2022.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1, Eurostat NUTS), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Italy", years = 2022)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Italy", years=[2022])
