Peru: election results by polling station

2006–2026 · 5 elections · PER

SAGE has andean parliament and legislative and presidential election results for Peru from 5 elections between 2006 and 2026: 2006, 2011, 2016, 2021 and 2026. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2026 election has 92,729 polling stations, and 89% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for 2021 and 2026.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Peru", years = 2026)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Peru", years=[2026])
