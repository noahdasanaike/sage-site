Iceland: election results by parliamentary constituency

1952–2024 · 28 elections · ISL

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Iceland from 28 elections between 1952 and 2024: 1952, 1959, 1963, 1967, 1968, 1971, 1974, 1978, 1979, 1980, 1983, 1987, 1988, 1991, 1995, 1996, 1999, 2003, 2004, 2007, 2009, 2012, 2013, 2016, 2017, 2020, 2021 and 2024. The smallest unit is the parliamentary constituency. The 2024 election has 6 parliamentary constituencies, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for 1959, 1963, 1967, 1971, 1974, 1978, 1979, 1983, 1987, 1991, 1995, 1999, 2003, 2004, 2007, 2009, 2012, 2013, 2016, 2017, 2020, 2021 and 2024.
