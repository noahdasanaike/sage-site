Luxembourg: election results by municipality

2004–2023 · 5 elections · LUX

SAGE has legislative election results for Luxembourg from 5 elections between 2004 and 2023: 2004, 2009, 2013, 2018 and 2023. The smallest unit is the municipality. The 2023 election has 100 municipalities, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1, Eurostat NUTS), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Luxembourg", years = 2023)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Luxembourg", years=[2023])
