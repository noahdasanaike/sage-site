Finland: election results by voting districts

2003–2024 · 9 elections · FIN

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Finland from 9 elections between 2003 and 2024: 2003, 2007, 2011, 2012, 2015, 2018, 2019, 2023 and 2024. The smallest unit is the voting districts (2019), municipality. The 2024 election has 1,756 units, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Finland", years = 2024)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Finland", years=[2024])
