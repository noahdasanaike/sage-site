Bulgaria: election results by polling station

2013–2026 · 9 elections · BGR

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Bulgaria from 9 elections between 2013 and 2026: 2013, 2014, 2016, 2017, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2026. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2026 election has 12,721 polling stations, and 94% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Bulgaria", years = 2026)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Bulgaria", years=[2026])
