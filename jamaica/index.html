Jamaica: election results by polling station

2007–2025 · 5 elections · JAM

SAGE has legislative election results for Jamaica from 5 elections between 2007 and 2025: 2007, 2011, 2016, 2020 and 2025. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2025 election has 7,294 polling stations, and 98% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.
