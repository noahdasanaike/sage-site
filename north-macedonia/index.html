North Macedonia: election results by polling station

2016–2024 · 2 elections · MKD

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for North Macedonia from 2 elections between 2016 and 2024: 2016 and 2024. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2024 election has 3,587 polling stations, and 97% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.
