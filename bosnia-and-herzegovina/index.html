Bosnia and Herzegovina: election results by polling station

2018–2022 · 2 elections · BIH

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Bosnia and Herzegovina from 2 elections between 2018 and 2022: 2018 and 2022. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2022 election has 5,557 polling stations, and 75% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Bosnia and Herzegovina", years = 2022)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Bosnia and Herzegovina", years=[2022])

Notes

  • Results are the House of Representatives of the Parliamentary Assembly of Bosnia and Herzegovina (Federation and Republika Srpska parts) and the three members of the Presidency, distinguished by special_type. NAME1 is the entity. Postal, absentee, and mobile-team ballots are counted centrally and are not included. Thiessen polygons are generated per contest over the territory that votes in it.
