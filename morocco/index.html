Morocco: election results by constituency

2016–2026 · 3 elections · MAR

SAGE has legislative election results for Morocco from 3 elections between 2016 and 2026: 2016, 2021 and 2026. The smallest unit is the constituency. The 2026 election has 92 constituencies, and 96% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

        sage::sage_load("Morocco", years = 2026)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Morocco", years=[2026])

Notes

  • Results are for the 92 local constituencies of the House of Representatives, with the same boundaries and seat counts in 2016, 2021 and 2026. The regional and national lists are reported only at region or national level and are not included.
  • Vote figures come from the Hespress election portal, which republishes the Ministry of the Interior's final results. The 2021 figures match the Ministry's own results file list for list, and the 2016 figures match Tafra's compilation. Hespress has no 2016 page for El Hajeb, so its 2016 lists come from Tafra.
  • total_votes is the sum of valid list votes in the constituency. Registered voters and invalid ballots are not published by constituency.
  • The 65 constituencies that are a whole province or prefecture use geoBoundaries ADM2 polygons. 23 split constituencies are unions of the OpenStreetMap communes and arrondissements named in Décret n° 2-11-603 (2011). Four split constituencies (Rabat-Océan, Rabat-Chellah, Marrakech-Ménara and Marrakech-Guéliz-Ennakhil) have Missing geometry because the decree divides them by units with no published boundaries.
  • Constituencies in Western Sahara are included. Laâyoune, Boujdour, Es-Semara, Oued Ed-Dahab and Aousserd lie wholly or almost wholly in the territory, and Tarfaya and Assa-Zag straddle its northern limit.
