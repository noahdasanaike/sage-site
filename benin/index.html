Benin: election results by constituency

2023–2026 · 2 elections · BEN

SAGE has legislative election results for Benin from 2 elections between 2023 and 2026: 2023 and 2026. The smallest unit is the constituency. The 2026 election has 24 constituencies, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.
