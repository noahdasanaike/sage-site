Timor-Leste: election results by administrative post

2018 · 1 election · TLS

SAGE has legislative election results for Timor-Leste from 2018. The smallest unit is the administrative post. The 2018 election has 65 administrative posts, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.
