France: election results by polling station

2002–2024 · 6 elections · FRA

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for France from 6 elections between 2002 and 2024: 2002, 2007, 2012, 2017, 2022 and 2024. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2024 election has 70,064 polling stations, and 95% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Notes

  • Missing several overseas commune boundaries (partially addressed for 2017, but not 2012 or before).
  • 2022 and 2024 boundaries are constructed from Thiessen polygons first run on each individual point, then grouped together by bureau to vote according to the home address to bureau de vote correspondence file published by INSEE. 2022 mappings are used for 2024; according to INSEE, “only 65 creations and 4 deletions of polling stations were observed between February 2023 and February 2024 (out of a total of 69,400 polling stations).” Note that this can produce enclaves which are nonetheless accurate. Thiessen polygons are only generated within, not across, municipalities; that is, the procedure is run one municipality at a time for all points assigned to the respective municipality. There are three exceptions: overseas departments of code 97, where I generate Thiessen from polling station addresses (type = “Thiessen”); units in Paris, which are reported exactly (type = “Actual”); and municipalities with only one polling station, which use the full municipality boundary (type = “Actual”).
  • Supplementary shapefiles for France (<= 2012) are provided at the NAME3_b (commune) level.
