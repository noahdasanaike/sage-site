Austria: election results by municipality

1999–2024 · 11 elections · AUT

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Austria from 11 elections between 1999 and 2024: 1999, 2002, 2006, 2008, 2010, 2013, 2016, 2017, 2019, 2022 and 2024. The smallest unit is the municipality. The 2024 election has 2,115 municipalities, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for 2010, 2013, 2016, 2017, 2019, 2022 and 2024.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Austria", years = 2024)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Austria", years=[2024])
