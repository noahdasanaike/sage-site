Israel: election results by polling station

2006–2022 · 8 elections · ISR

SAGE has legislative election results for Israel from 8 elections between 2006 and 2022: 2006, 2009, 2013, 2015, 2019, 2020, 2021 and 2022. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2022 election has 11,707 polling stations. Unit boundaries are available for 2020 and 2021.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Israel", years = 2022)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Israel", years=[2022])
