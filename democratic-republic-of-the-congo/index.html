Democratic Republic of the Congo: election results by polling station

2023 · 1 election · COD

SAGE has presidential election results for the Democratic Republic of the Congo from 2023. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2023 election has 20,104 polling stations, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

        sage.sage_load("Democratic Republic of the Congo", years=[2023])
