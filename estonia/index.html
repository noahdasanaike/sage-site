Estonia: election results by polling station

2011–2023 · 4 elections · EST

SAGE has legislative election results for Estonia from 4 elections between 2011 and 2023: 2011, 2015, 2019 and 2023. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2023 election has 504 polling stations, and 80% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for 2019 and 2023.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1, Eurostat NUTS), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Estonia", years = 2023)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Estonia", years=[2023])
