Suriname: election results by polling station

2025 · 1 election · SUR

SAGE has legislative election results for Suriname from 2025. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2025 election has 431 polling stations, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.
