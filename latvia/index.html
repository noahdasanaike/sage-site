Latvia: election results by polling station

2014–2026 · 4 elections · LVA

SAGE has legislative election results for Latvia from 4 elections between 2014 and 2026: 2014, 2018, 2022 and 2026. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2026 election has 939 polling stations, and 99% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Latvia", years = 2026)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Latvia", years=[2026])
