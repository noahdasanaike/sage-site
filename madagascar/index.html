Madagascar: election results by polling station

2018–2024 · 3 elections · MDG

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Madagascar from 3 elections between 2018 and 2024: 2018, 2023 and 2024. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2024 election has 27,540 polling stations, and 96% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.
