Greece: election results by polling station

2012–2023 · 4 elections · GRC

SAGE has legislative election results for Greece from 4 elections between 2012 and 2023: 2012, 2015, 2019 and 2023. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2023 election has 23,338 polling stations, and 92% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1, Eurostat NUTS), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Greece", years = 2023)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Greece", years=[2023])
