Lebanon: election results by polling station

2018–2022 · 2 elections · LBN

SAGE has legislative election results for Lebanon from 2 elections between 2018 and 2022: 2018 and 2022. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2022 election has 6,860 polling stations, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Notes

  • "Abroad" special type inferred using place names in NAME3, some of which are locations definitively outside Lebanon. The 2022 election publishes no polling-station coordinates, so 2022 stations are located by two routes recorded in geometry_type_b: reuse of a 2018 station coordinate where the station recurs, and fresh geocoding of the building name otherwise. Of 6,860 in-country units, 4,937 reuse a 2018 station and 556 are freshly geocoded to the building (80.1% station level); the remaining 1,367 fall back to a locality centroid (1,154 from 2018, 213 geocoded) because the source names the venue only generically, e.g. "the public school". Every in-country 2022 unit has a location; rows still marked Missing are out-of-country ("Abroad") voting, which has no location in Lebanon by construction.
