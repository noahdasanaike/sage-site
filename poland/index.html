Poland: election results by polling station

1990–2025 · 16 elections · POL

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Poland from 16 elections between 1990 and 2025: 1990, 1991, 1993, 1995, 1997, 2000, 2001, 2005, 2007, 2010, 2011, 2015, 2019, 2020, 2023 and 2025. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2025 election has 32,140 polling stations, and 98% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1, Eurostat NUTS), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Poland", years = 2025)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Poland", years=[2025])

Notes

  • Thiessen polygons are generated at the address level, with multiple precincts per address.
  • Legislative election results are reported for Sejm lists rather than individual parties.
