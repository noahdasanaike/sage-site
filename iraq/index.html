Iraq: election results by constituency

2021 · 1 election · IRQ

SAGE has legislative election results for Iraq from 2021. The smallest unit is the constituency. The 2021 election has 92 constituencies, and 90% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Iraq", years = 2021)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Iraq", years=[2021])
