Hungary: election results by polling station

2002–2026 · 7 elections · HUN

SAGE has legislative election results for Hungary from 7 elections between 2002 and 2026: 2002, 2006, 2010, 2014, 2018, 2022 and 2026. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2026 election has 10,022 polling stations, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for 2010, 2014, 2018, 2022 and 2026.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Hungary", years = 2026)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Hungary", years=[2026])
