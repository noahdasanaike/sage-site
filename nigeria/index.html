Nigeria: election results by parliamentary constituency

2019 · 1 election · NGA

SAGE has legislative election results for Nigeria from 2019. The smallest unit is the parliamentary constituency. The 2019 election has 337 parliamentary constituencies, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Nigeria", years = 2019)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Nigeria", years=[2019])
