Kosovo: election results by polling station

2017–2026 · 5 elections · XKX

SAGE has legislative election results for Kosovo from 5 elections between 2017 and 2026: 2017, 2019, 2021, 2025 and 2026. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2026 election has 2,498 polling stations, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Kosovo", years = 2026)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Kosovo", years=[2026])
