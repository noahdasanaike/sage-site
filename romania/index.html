Romania: election results by polling station

2014–2025 · 6 elections · ROU

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Romania from 6 elections between 2014 and 2025: 2014, 2016, 2019, 2020, 2024 and 2025. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2025 election has 19,944 polling stations, and 95% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for 2014, 2019, 2020, 2024 and 2025.
