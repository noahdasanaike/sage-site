Sweden: election results by electoral district

2002–2026 · 7 elections · SWE

SAGE has legislative election results for Sweden from 7 elections between 2002 and 2026: 2002, 2006, 2010, 2014, 2018, 2022 and 2026. The smallest unit is the electoral district. The 2026 election has 6,626 electoral districts, and 95% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for 2006, 2010, 2014, 2018, 2022 and 2026.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1, Eurostat NUTS), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Sweden", years = 2026)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Sweden", years=[2026])
