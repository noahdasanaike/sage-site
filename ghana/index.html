Ghana: election results by parliamentary constituency

2012–2024 · 4 elections · GHA

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Ghana from 4 elections between 2012 and 2024: 2012, 2016, 2020 and 2024. The smallest unit is the parliamentary constituency. The 2024 election has 276 parliamentary constituencies, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Ghana", years = 2024)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Ghana", years=[2024])
