Sri Lanka: election results by polling division

2020–2024 · 2 elections · LKA

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Sri Lanka from 2 elections between 2020 and 2024: 2020 and 2024. The smallest unit is the polling division. The 2024 election has 160 polling divisions, and 96% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Sri Lanka", years = 2024)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Sri Lanka", years=[2024])
