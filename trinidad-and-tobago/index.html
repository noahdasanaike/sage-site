Trinidad and Tobago: election results by parliamentary constituency

2015–2025 · 3 elections · TTO

SAGE has legislative election results for Trinidad and Tobago from 3 elections between 2015 and 2025: 2015, 2020 and 2025. The smallest unit is the parliamentary constituency. The 2025 election has 41 parliamentary constituencies, and 88% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Trinidad and Tobago", years = 2025)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Trinidad and Tobago", years=[2025])
