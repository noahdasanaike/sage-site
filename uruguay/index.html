Uruguay: election results by polling station

2014–2024 · 3 elections · URY

SAGE has legislative election results for Uruguay from 3 elections between 2014 and 2024: 2014, 2019 and 2024. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2024 election has 7,358 polling stations, and 99% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Uruguay", years=[2024])
