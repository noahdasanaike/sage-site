Bhutan: election results by polling station

2018 · 1 election · BTN

SAGE has legislative election results for Bhutan from 2018. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2018 election has 865 polling stations, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Bhutan", years = 2018)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Bhutan", years=[2018])
