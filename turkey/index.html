Turkey: election results by polling station

2011–2023 · 5 elections · TUR

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Turkey from 5 elections between 2011 and 2023: 2011, 2014, 2015, 2018 and 2023. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2023 election has 199,084 polling stations, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.
