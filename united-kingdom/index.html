United Kingdom: election results by parliamentary constituency

2001–2024 · 7 elections · GBR

SAGE has legislative election results for the United Kingdom from 7 elections between 2001 and 2024: 2001, 2005, 2010, 2015, 2017, 2019 and 2024. The smallest unit is the parliamentary constituency. The 2024 election has 650 parliamentary constituencies, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.
