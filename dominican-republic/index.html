Dominican Republic: election results by polling station

2000–2024 · 10 elections · DOM

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for the Dominican Republic from 10 elections between 2000 and 2024: 2000, 2002, 2004, 2006, 2008, 2010, 2012, 2016, 2020 and 2024. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2024 election has 16,945 polling stations, and 96% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for 2002, 2004, 2006, 2008, 2012, 2020 and 2024.
