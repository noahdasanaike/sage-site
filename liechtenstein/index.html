Liechtenstein: election results by municipality

2001–2025 · 7 elections · LIE

SAGE has legislative election results for Liechtenstein from 7 elections between 2001 and 2025: 2001, 2005, 2009, 2013, 2017, 2021 and 2025. The smallest unit is the municipality. The 2025 election has 11 municipalities, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Liechtenstein", years = 2025)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Liechtenstein", years=[2025])
