Canada: election results by polling station

1997–2025 · 10 elections · CAN

SAGE has legislative election results for Canada from 10 elections between 1997 and 2025: 1997, 2000, 2004, 2006, 2008, 2011, 2015, 2019, 2021 and 2025. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2025 election has 74,226 polling stations, and 99% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for 2000, 2004, 2006, 2008, 2011, 2015, 2019, 2021 and 2025.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Canada", years = 2025)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Canada", years=[2025])
