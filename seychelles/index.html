Seychelles: election results by district

2020–2025 · 2 elections · SYC

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for the Seychelles from 2 elections between 2020 and 2025: 2020 and 2025. The smallest unit is the district. The 2025 election has 26 districts, and 96% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.
