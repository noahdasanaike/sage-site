Albania: election results by polling station

2017–2025 · 3 elections · ALB

SAGE has legislative election results for Albania from 3 elections between 2017 and 2025: 2017, 2021 and 2025. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2025 election has 5,218 polling stations, and 97% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for 2021 and 2025.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1, Eurostat NUTS), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Albania", years = 2025)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Albania", years=[2025])
