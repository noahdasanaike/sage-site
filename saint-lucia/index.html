Saint Lucia: election results by polling district

2021–2025 · 2 elections · LCA

SAGE has legislative election results for Saint Lucia from 2 elections between 2021 and 2025: 2021 and 2025. The smallest unit is the polling district. The 2025 election has 110 polling districts, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Notes

  • Results are reported at the polling-box level (boxes A1(a), A1(b), etc.) within each constituency. Polling boxes are not individually geolocated, so each box is assigned its constituency's polygon and centroid and is therefore flagged as a geocode duplicate.
  • Constituency polygons are constructed from OCHA cod-ab settlement boundaries: each settlement is assigned to a constituency by matching its name to voter addresses in the electoral register and taking the majority registered constituency, unmatched settlements are filled by the nearest matched settlement within the same quarter, and settlements are then unioned per constituency. The split-quarter seats (the five Castries constituencies and the Gros Islet/Babonneau, Vieux Fort, Micoud, and Dennery pairs) carry some boundary-reconstruction uncertainty because their settlement-to-seat assignment is inferred from voter registrations rather than an official schedule.
