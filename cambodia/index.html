Cambodia: election results by commune

2013 · 1 election · KHM

SAGE has legislative election results for Cambodia from 2013. The smallest unit is the commune. The 2013 election has 1,621 communes, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Cambodia", years = 2013)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Cambodia", years=[2013])
