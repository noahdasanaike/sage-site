Panama: election results by electoral circuit

2004–2024 · 5 elections · PAN

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Panama from 5 elections between 2004 and 2024: 2004, 2009, 2014, 2019 and 2024. The smallest unit is the electoral circuit. The 2024 election has 39 electoral circuits, and 95% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Panama", years = 2024)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Panama", years=[2024])
