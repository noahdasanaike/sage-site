Russia: election results by polling station

2000–2024 · 11 elections · RUS

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Russia from 11 elections between 2000 and 2024: 2000, 2003, 2004, 2007, 2008, 2011, 2012, 2016, 2018, 2021 and 2024. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2024 election has 91,530 polling stations, and 97% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for 2012, 2016, 2018, 2021 and 2024.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Russia", years = 2024)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Russia", years=[2024])

Notes

  • Systematic missingness of polling station coordinates by region means that Thiessen polygons are generated separately by region by year. This is also necessary to address Russian annexation of Crimea and Sevastopol.
  • Tens of polling stations reported as belonging to Moscow Oblast in 2012 actually fall within the bounds of the Moscow federal subject; the region was manually adjusted to account for this discrepancy.
