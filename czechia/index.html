Czechia: election results by election precinct

2002–2025 · 9 elections · CZE

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Czechia from 9 elections between 2002 and 2025: 2002, 2006, 2010, 2013, 2017, 2018, 2021, 2023 and 2025. The smallest unit is the election precinct. The 2025 election has 14,819 election precincts, and 99% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for 2017, 2018, 2021, 2023 and 2025.
