South Korea: election results by polling station

2002–2025 · 11 elections · KOR

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for South Korea from 11 elections between 2002 and 2025: 2002, 2004, 2007, 2008, 2012, 2016, 2017, 2020, 2022, 2024 and 2025. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2025 election has 14,295 polling stations, and 98% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for 2007, 2008, 2012, 2016, 2017, 2020, 2022, 2024 and 2025.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("South Korea", years = 2025)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("South Korea", years=[2025])
