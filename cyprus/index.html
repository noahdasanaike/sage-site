Cyprus: election results by polling station

2001–2026 · 11 elections · CYP

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Cyprus from 11 elections between 2001 and 2026: 2001, 2003, 2006, 2008, 2011, 2013, 2016, 2018, 2021, 2023 and 2026. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2026 election has 1,280 polling stations, and 98% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.
