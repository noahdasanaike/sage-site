Bolivia: election results by polling station

2019–2025 · 3 elections · BOL

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Bolivia from 3 elections between 2019 and 2025: 2019, 2020 and 2025. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2025 election has 34,026 polling stations, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Bolivia", years = 2025)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Bolivia", years=[2025])
