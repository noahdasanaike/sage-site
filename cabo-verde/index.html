Cabo Verde: election results by polling station

2021–2026 · 2 elections · CPV

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Cabo Verde from 2 elections between 2021 and 2026: 2021 and 2026. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2026 election has 1,057 polling stations, and 95% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.
