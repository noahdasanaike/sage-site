Croatia: election results by polling station

2011–2025 · 8 elections · HRV

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Croatia from 8 elections between 2011 and 2025: 2011, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2019, 2020, 2024 and 2025. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2025 election has 6,755 polling stations, and 95% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1, Eurostat NUTS), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Croatia", years = 2025)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Croatia", years=[2025])

Notes

  • Party coalitions in the "party" column follow the same ordering as in the original data ("party_b"), leading to duplicate coalitions of different party orderings.
  • Several party name-party acronym correspondences are assumed due to variability in reporting as coalition members.
  • The _p suffix on geometry columns refers to latitude-longitude geocodings on individual polling stations, whereas geometry is provided at a higher level in the supplementary shapefiles.
