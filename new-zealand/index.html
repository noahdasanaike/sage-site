New Zealand: election results by polling station

1996–2023 · 10 elections · NZL

SAGE has legislative election results for New Zealand from 10 elections between 1996 and 2023: 1996, 1999, 2002, 2005, 2008, 2011, 2014, 2017, 2020 and 2023. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2023 election has 4,051 polling stations, and 84% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

        sage::sage_load("New Zealand", years = 2023)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("New Zealand", years=[2023])
