Malaysia: election results by polling district

2008–2022 · 4 elections · MYS

SAGE has legislative election results for Malaysia from 4 elections between 2008 and 2022: 2008, 2013, 2018 and 2022. The smallest unit is the polling district. The 2022 election has 38,349 polling districts, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Malaysia", years = 2022)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Malaysia", years=[2022])

Notes

  • Malaysian coalition results align with Wikipedia aggregates, but there seem to be discrepancies in the vote-shares attained by the parties within each coalition.
  • Beginning with the 2013 election, results are provided at the saluran (polling-stream) level: each polling district (daerah mengundi) is divided into several streams that share one polling centre and the same geometry. The party column gives the ballot-symbol party (the coalition for BN/PH/PN/GPS or a standalone party), with the component party in party_b and the coalition in party_c. The 2008 election remains at parliamentary-constituency level. Polling-district polygons are Thiessen cells built from the district points (Tindak Malaysia daerah-mengundi centroids where available, otherwise Google-geocoded polling centres), because gazetted polling-district boundaries are not public for Sarawak and do not tile the country's unpopulated interior.
