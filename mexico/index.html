Mexico: election results by polling station

1991–2024 · 12 elections · MEX

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Mexico from 12 elections between 1991 and 2024: 1991, 1994, 1997, 2000, 2003, 2006, 2009, 2012, 2015, 2018, 2021 and 2024. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2024 election has 167,891 polling stations, and 99% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for 2006, 2009, 2015, 2018 and 2024.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Mexico", years = 2024)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Mexico", years=[2024])
