Lithuania: election results by precinct

2016–2024 · 4 elections · LTU

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Lithuania from 4 elections between 2016 and 2024: 2016, 2019, 2020 and 2024. The smallest unit is the precinct. The 2024 election has 1,947 precincts, and 97% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1, Eurostat NUTS), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Lithuania", years = 2024)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Lithuania", years=[2024])
