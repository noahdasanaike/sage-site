Ireland: election results by parliamentary constituency

2002–2025 · 7 elections · IRL

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Ireland from 7 elections between 2002 and 2025: 2002, 2007, 2011, 2016, 2020, 2024 and 2025. The smallest unit is the parliamentary constituency. The 2025 election has 43 parliamentary constituencies, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for 2016, 2020, 2024 and 2025.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1, Eurostat NUTS), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Ireland", years = 2025)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Ireland", years=[2025])
