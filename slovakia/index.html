Slovakia: election results by polling station

2002–2024 · 9 elections · SVK

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Slovakia from 9 elections between 2002 and 2024: 2002, 2006, 2010, 2012, 2016, 2019, 2020, 2023 and 2024. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2024 election has 5,938 polling stations, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Slovakia", years = 2024)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Slovakia", years=[2024])
