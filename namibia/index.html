Namibia: election results by parliamentary constituency

2014–2024 · 3 elections · NAM

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Namibia from 3 elections between 2014 and 2024: 2014, 2019 and 2024. The smallest unit is the parliamentary constituency. The 2024 election has 121 parliamentary constituencies, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.
