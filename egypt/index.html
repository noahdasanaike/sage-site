Egypt: election results by polling station

2014–2018 · 3 elections · EGY

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Egypt from 3 elections between 2014 and 2018: 2014, 2015 and 2018. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2018 election has 13,706 polling stations, and 97% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.
