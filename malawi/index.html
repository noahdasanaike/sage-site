Malawi: election results by polling station

2019–2025 · 2 elections · MWI

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Malawi from 2 elections between 2019 and 2025: 2019 and 2025. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2025 election has 15,148 polling stations, and 98% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Malawi", years = 2025)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Malawi", years=[2025])
