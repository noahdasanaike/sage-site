Switzerland: election results by municipality

1971–2023 · 14 elections · CHE

SAGE has legislative election results for Switzerland from 14 elections between 1971 and 2023: 1971, 1975, 1979, 1983, 1987, 1991, 1995, 1999, 2003, 2007, 2011, 2015, 2019 and 2023. The smallest unit is the municipality. The 2023 election has 2,131 municipalities, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for 2011, 2015, 2019 and 2023.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Switzerland", years = 2023)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Switzerland", years=[2023])
