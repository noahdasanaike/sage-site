Costa Rica: election results by polling station

2018–2022 · 2 elections · CRI

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Costa Rica from 2 elections between 2018 and 2022: 2018 and 2022. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2022 election has 2,152 polling stations, and 98% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Costa Rica", years = 2022)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Costa Rica", years=[2022])
