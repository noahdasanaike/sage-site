Japan: election results by municipality

2009–2026 · 7 elections · JPN

SAGE has legislative election results for Japan from 7 elections between 2009 and 2026: 2009, 2012, 2014, 2017, 2021, 2024 and 2026. The smallest unit is the municipality. The 2026 election has 1,926 municipalities, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Japan", years = 2026)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Japan", years=[2026])
