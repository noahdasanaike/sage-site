Greenland: election results by settlements

2005–2026 · 13 elections · GRL

SAGE has danish parliament and legislative election results for Greenland from 13 elections between 2005 and 2026: 2005, 2007, 2009, 2011, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2018, 2019, 2021, 2022, 2025 and 2026. The smallest unit is the settlements. The 2026 election has 73 settlementses, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Greenland", years = 2026)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Greenland", years=[2026])
