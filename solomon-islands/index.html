Solomon Islands: election results by parliamentary constituency

2006–2019 · 4 elections · SLB

SAGE has legislative election results for the Solomon Islands from 4 elections between 2006 and 2019: 2006, 2010, 2014 and 2019. The smallest unit is the parliamentary constituency. The 2019 election has 50 parliamentary constituencies, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Solomon Islands", years = 2019)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Solomon Islands", years=[2019])
