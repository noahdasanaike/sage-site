Kyrgyzstan: election results by polling station

2015–2025 · 5 elections · KGZ

SAGE has legislative and presidential election results for Kyrgyzstan from 5 elections between 2015 and 2025: 2015, 2017, 2020, 2021 and 2025. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2025 election has 2,492 polling stations, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Kyrgyzstan", years = 2025)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Kyrgyzstan", years=[2025])

Notes

  • Kyrgyzstan has an "against all" vote that is named accordingly in the party column.
