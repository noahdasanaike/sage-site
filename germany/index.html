Germany: election results by polling station

1998–2025 · 8 elections · DEU

SAGE has legislative election results for Germany from 8 elections between 1998 and 2025: 1998, 2002, 2005, 2009, 2013, 2017, 2021 and 2025. The smallest unit is the polling station. The 2025 election has 93,948 polling stations, and 98% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Germany", years = 2025)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Germany", years=[2025])
