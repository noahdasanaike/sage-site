Belize: election results by constituency

1984–2025 · 10 elections · BLZ

SAGE has legislative election results for Belize from 10 elections between 1984 and 2025: 1984, 1989, 1993, 1998, 2003, 2008, 2012, 2015, 2020 and 2025. The smallest unit is the constituency. The 2025 election has 29 constituencies, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Belize", years = 2025)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Belize", years=[2025])
