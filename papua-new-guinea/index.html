Papua New Guinea: election results by electorate

1987–2017 · 7 elections · PNG

SAGE has legislative election results for Papua New Guinea from 7 elections between 1987 and 2017: 1987, 1992, 1997, 2002, 2007, 2012 and 2017. The smallest unit is the electorate. The 2017 election has 89 electorates, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

The same results added up within administrative areas (GADM 4.1), all years in one file per level. You can also map them.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Papua New Guinea", years = 2017)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Papua New Guinea", years=[2017])
