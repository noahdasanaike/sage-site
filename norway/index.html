Norway: election results by municipality

2001–2025 · 7 elections · NOR

SAGE has legislative election results for Norway from 7 elections between 2001 and 2025: 2001, 2005, 2009, 2013, 2017, 2021 and 2025. The smallest unit is the municipality (<= 2013), electoral district (>= 2017). The 2025 election has 3,602 units, and 45% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Download

csv opens in Excel, Stata, R, or Python. gpkg has the unit boundaries for QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python. parquet has the vote rows as Parquet. rds is the native R sf object. Each ZIP has a README with the columns.

See the download section on the main page.

In R or Python:

R:      install.packages("sage")
        sage::sage_load("Norway", years = 2025)

Python: import sage
        sage.sage_load("Norway", years=[2025])
