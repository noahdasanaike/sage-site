South Africa: election results by voting districts

2004–2024 · 5 elections · ZAF

SAGE has legislative election results for South Africa from 5 elections between 2004 and 2024: 2004, 2009, 2014, 2019 and 2024. The smallest unit is the voting districts. The 2024 election has 23,291 voting districtses, and 100% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.
