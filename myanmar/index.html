Myanmar: election results by parliamentary constituency

2010–2015 · 2 elections · MMR

SAGE has legislative election results for Myanmar from 2 elections between 2010 and 2015: 2010 and 2015. The smallest unit is the parliamentary constituency. The 2015 election has 322 parliamentary constituencies, and 97% of them have a location. Unit boundaries are available for every election.

Elections

Units are the smallest units in the data, and located units are those with coordinates. A year with several rows has several contests or votes; filter on them before you add up votes.
